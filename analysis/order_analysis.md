# 1. Delivery Performance Analysis 
The olist dataset contains 99,441 orders, of which 96,476 had a recorded customer delivery data. The reamining 2,965 orders were exlcuded from the delivery performance anlysis because they did not have a recorded delivery date. 

Delivery performance was measured by vomparing the actual delivery date with the estimated delivery date. Both timestamps were normalized to calendar dates to avoid classify orders based on difference in delivery and delivery times within the same day.

## The result showed that: 
## 1. 91.89% of delivered orders arrived early 
## 2. 6.77% arrived late 
## 3. 1.34% arrived on the estimated delivery date 
These preliminary finding suggest that Olist generally delivered orders earlier than promised.
